# 📱 Complete Device Reset Checklist

A **step-by-step, organized guide** to safely back up all your data before resetting your device — so nothing important is lost.

---

## 🔹 Step 1: System Preparation

* Open **Settings → Software Update**
* Check for available updates
* Update your device before proceeding

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Create a Backup Space

Use any one of the following:

* **Telegram Saved Messages** or create a private channel named: `Data Backup Before Reset`
* Another phone (via Xender / ShareIt)
* Cloud storage

📌 Use this space to save:

* Screenshots
* APK files
* Important documents
* Notes

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Browser Data

* Log in to all browsers using your **Google account**
* Check shopping sites (Amazon, Flipkart)

  * If not logged in → cart data will be lost
* If using **Kiwi Browser**:

  * Take screenshots of saved extensions

✅ **Tip:** Always use browsers signed in with Gmail for auto-sync

---

## 🔹 Step 4: Contacts & Call History

* Sync contacts with **Google Account**
* OR export contacts to SD card

📌 Recommended:

* Install **Google Contacts** for auto-sync
* Install **Call History Backup & Restore** (call logs often don’t sync automatically)

---

## 🔹 Step 5: Messages (SMS)

* Google Messages may not always sync
* Install **SMS Organizer by Microsoft**
* Enable cloud backup using Gmail

---

## 🔹 Step 6: Calendar

* Use **Google Calendar** only
* Ensure sync is ON

---

## 🔹 Step 7: Clock & Alarms

* Take screenshots of:

  * Alarms
  * Timers
  * Clock settings

Save screenshots to:

* Telegram
* Google Drive

---

## 🔹 Step 8: File Manager

Backup files using:

* Google Drive (15GB) 
* Terabox (1TB) - for non sensitive files
* Telegram Saved Messages - safe

📌 Large files → transfer using another phone

---

## 🔹 Step 9: Notes

* Backup notes to:

  * Telegram
  * Google Keep (recommended)

✅ Always use **Google Keep** for automatic sync

---

## 🔹 Step 10: Media Apps

* Check **YouTube downloads**
* Check **MX Player downloads**

---

## 🔹 Step 11: Document Apps

Check apps like:

* CamScanner / Doc Scanner
* WPS Office
* iLovePDF

Backup all documents manually

---

## 🔹 Step 12: Notes & Productivity Apps

Check apps such as:

* SimpleMind
* Mindly
* Squid Notes
* Clipboard managers
* Moon+ Reader
* Sectograph

📌 Take screenshots of:

* Notes
* Saved content
* App settings

---

## 🔹 Step 13: Educational Apps

Examples:

* Mimo
* Programming Hub
* SoloLearn
* Enki

📸 Screenshot:

* Login info
* Course progress

---

## 🔹 Step 14: Virtual Machine Apps

Apps like:

* VMOS Pro
* Virtual Android
* Sandbox
* VMware

✔ Save:

* Installed apps
* VM data
* Screenshots of configurations

---

## 🔹 Step 15: WhatsApp Backup

* Backup chats to **Google Drive**
* Save:

  * About text
  * Profile name
  * Custom font style

---

## 🔹 Final Safety Steps

### ✅ Extra Important Tips

1. Add your Google account to another phone (disable sync there)
2. Log in to Telegram on another device
3. Screenshot your full app list
4. Zip important folders before transfer
5. Go to:

   * Settings → Google → Backup
   * Turn ON backup
   * Turn OFF photos/videos if large
6. Click **Backup Now**

---

## 🔹 Final Step: Reset Device

Go to:

**Settings → Reset → Backup & Reset → Backup My Data (ON) → Factory Reset**

Confirm and wait for completion.

---

## ⚠️ Important Warning

✔ Always sign in using **Gmail** for trusted apps
❌ Avoid Facebook login

Login order recommendation:
**Temp Mail → Proton Mail → Gmail → Phone Number → Facebook**

---

## ✅ Final Note

⚠️ Be careful before resetting.
Once erased, data cannot be recovered.
